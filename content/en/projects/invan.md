## Context

**tiin** is a supermarket chain in Tashkent. Its in-house IT department built **Invan** — a retail automation platform used in tiin stores and sold to other businesses: point-of-sale, warehouse stock, customer and supplier records, receipts and labels, with integrations for Payme, Uzum, the OFD.uz virtual cash register and product labelling (Markirovka). According to invan.uz, more than 100 companies use it.

## What I worked on

- **Admin panel** — modules of the large back-office app that store managers use every day: orders, products, warehouse stock, clients, analytics charts, notifications and settings, plus Excel export for reports.
- **Landing pages** — the public [invan.uz](https://invan.uz/) site: hero, POS hardware, integrations, blog, pricing and contact sections, fully responsive.
- **Maintenance** — fixing bugs in production projects and adding new modules on request from the business.

## Stack

Vue.js, Vuex, Vue Router, Axios, Bootstrap-Vue, Chart.js, Sass.

## What I learned

Building software for a business that uses it in its own stores gives very short feedback loops — cashiers and managers report problems the same day, so reliability and simple UI matter more than anything else.
