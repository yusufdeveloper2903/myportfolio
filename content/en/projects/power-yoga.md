## Problem

Power Yoga offers many courses — back care, core, morning practice and more. Visitors needed to
understand the offering quickly and pick the right class.

## What I built

- A landing page that presents courses by theme.
- Clear schedules and calls to action for recurring classes and single sessions.
- A responsive, lightweight layout.
