## The product

Hunarda is an innovative education platform designed to transform the traditional learning experience. With three key roles — **administrator, teacher and student** — it simplifies educational processes and promotes collaboration.

- **Administrators** have full control over the platform: they manage user accounts and system settings to keep access secure.
- **Teachers** create and manage courses, upload materials and engage with students through discussions and feedback.
- **Students** enroll in courses, access materials and interact with teachers and peers.

## What I built

I developed the admin panel in Vue.js:

- **Dashboard & statistics** — platform totals and monthly charts for income, sales, courses and students, filterable by category, course and date.
- **Users** — staff with roles and students with points, filters by category and course, and Excel export.
- **Course management** — a directory of categories, courses, archive, tags and promo codes, plus tariffs.
- **Finance** — payments through Click, Payme, Uzum, Paynet and cash, filtered by method, status, date, course and promo code.
- **Chat** — teacher–student conversations the administrator can review.
- **Content** — an “About us” section with FAQ, project pitch, terms and contact pages.
- **UX** — light and dark themes, a multilingual interface and paginated tables throughout.
