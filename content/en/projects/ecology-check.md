## The product

Ecology Check is a project designed to help assess the ecology of a country. It offers various features to work easily with ecological data:

- Users **collect data** on biodiversity, air and water quality, and land use.
- The project **analyses** this data to identify patterns and trends.
- The data is **visualised** through maps, charts and graphs.
- Users **generate reports** summarising key findings and collaborate with others.
- **Alerts and notifications** help identify ecological issues in time.
- An **integration gateway** connects other data sources for seamless data sharing.

The platform brings together state agencies — the ecology committee, forestry, geology, hydrometeorology, agriculture, water management, health, emergencies and environmental laboratories — each with its own workspace.

## What I built

- **Agency workspaces** — a dashboard of agencies, each with its own results, statistics and data entry.
- **Maps** — an interactive map of Uzbekistan and thematic maps (concentrations, water resources, flora and fauna, protected areas, air quality) with a **heatmap of pollution sources** that animates indicators over time.
- **Analytics & statistics** — indicator tables with date and indicator filters and bar charts by year.
- **Data entry** — monitoring objects and laboratory indicators by region for air, water and land, with CSV export.
- **Reference data** — classifiers for groups, units, ownership forms, pollutant types, regions and parameters, plus settings and a knowledge base.
