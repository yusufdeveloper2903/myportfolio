## Problem

True movie lovers are rarely satisfied with just watching a film. They want to know everything
around it — the cast, the inspiration behind the story and the latest news.

## What I built

- A catalogue of movies with posters, ratings and release dates.
- Detail pages with cast and story information.
- A responsive layout that works from small phones to wide desktop screens.

## What I learned

Working with an external movie API taught me to design around loading and error states and to keep
data fetching separate from presentation.
