## The product

**Quramiz.uz** is SQB bank’s marketplace for construction and renovation materials in Uzbekistan: building materials, tools, electrics, plumbing, interior finishing and more, with delivery across Tashkent and the regions and the option to buy on credit. It is backed by a mobile app for iOS and Android.

## My role

Together with a teammate I built the user interface of the web store and took it live — this was my first production project, as an intern on SQB’s team.

- **Catalog & navigation** — a mega-menu of categories, category pages with price and brand filters and sorting, brand pages and search.
- **Product experience** — product cards with stock and unit pricing, a product page with description, specifications, reviews and offers from different suppliers, plus favourites and cart.
- **Home page** — promo banners, “season”, “new” and “you may need” carousels, brands and the mobile app section.
- **Responsive UI** — a dedicated mobile layout with bottom navigation.

## Stack

Vue 2, Vuex, Vue Router, Axios and Yandex Maps, on top of a Laravel backend.

## What I learned

Shipping a real e-commerce UI taught me component thinking — the same product card powers carousels, category grids and search — and how much small details like stock, units and prices matter to buyers.
