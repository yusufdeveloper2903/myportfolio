## Problem

Medical information online is often hard to read and hard to trust. The goal was a calm, clear
interface that makes complex drug and health information approachable.

## What I built

- A content-focused layout with strong typography and clear hierarchy.
- Reusable sections for treatments, articles and calls to action.
- A fully responsive design.

## What I learned

Readability is a feature: spacing, line length and contrast matter as much as functionality.
