## Problem

Businesses such as banks and retailers need one admin panel for customers, schedules and analytics — with different permissions for different staff.

## What I built

- **Modular architecture** — each domain lives in its own module with its own routes, plugged into the app router.
- **Authentication & permissions** — JWT auth service with role-based access control powered by CASL.
- **Rich UI** — data tables with pagination, calendars (FullCalendar), charts (ApexCharts, ECharts), maps (Leaflet), rich-text editing and validated forms (Vuelidate).
- **Layouts** — vertical and horizontal navigation, collapsible sidebar, light/dark theme and i18n.
- **Quality** — TypeScript, Pinia stores with tests (Vitest, Pinia testing), strict ESLint rules.

## Stack

Vue 3 (Composition API), TypeScript, Pinia, Vite, PrimeVue, Tailwind CSS.
