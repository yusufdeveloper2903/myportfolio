When I started my Telegram channel, [Basics of Programming](https://t.me/basics_of_programming),
I thought I was doing it for others. It turned out I was the one learning the most.

## Repetition makes knowledge stick

Explaining a topic forces you to go back to the basics. Every post was a chance to revisit
JavaScript, Vue.js, HTML, CSS and Sass — and to remember details I had started to forget.

## Explaining reveals your mistakes

You only notice the gaps in your understanding when you try to explain something simply.
Writing for an audience made me check my assumptions and correct my own mistakes.

## Takeaway

If you want to grow as an engineer, teach what you know. Write it down, share it and let
questions from others show you what to learn next.
